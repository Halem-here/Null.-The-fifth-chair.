Why Lora and not Source Serif 4: Source Serif 4 could not be obtained in the build environment (network access to
font sources was blocked), so a comparable free serif that was available locally was used instead.

Checked again at the final review pass (2026-10-15): the build machine's filesystem was searched and holds no Source Serif 4 font files, so Lora remains the heading font. If you add the real files to this folder, point the heading @font-face in css/style.css at them and add them here.
